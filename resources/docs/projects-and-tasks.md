# Projects and Tasks

Projects group related work. Each project contains tasks, which move through the columns of its board.

## Creating a task

1. Open a project and click **Add task** at the bottom of any column.
2. Enter a title and press **Enter**.
3. Click the task to add a description, assignee, due date, and labels.

## Assigning tasks and setting due dates

Open a task and use the **Assignee** field to choose one workspace member. Each task has one assignee. Use **Due date** to pick a date; overdue tasks show a red date on the board, and the assignee receives a reminder email the day before the task is due.

## Customizing board columns

New projects start with **To do**, **In progress**, and **Done** columns. Project Admins can click **+** to add a column, drag a column to reorder it, or open the column menu to rename or delete it. A column must be empty before it can be deleted.

## Adding subtasks and attachments

In an open task, click **Add subtask** to break the work into a checklist. Drag files onto the task to attach them. Each attachment can be up to 100 MB on the Team plan and 25 MB on the Free plan.

## Archiving and restoring projects

Open **Project settings > Archive project** to hide a finished project from the dashboard. Archived projects are read-only. To bring one back, go to **Settings > Archived projects** and click **Restore**.

## Deleting tasks and projects

Deleted tasks and projects stay in **Settings > Trash** for 30 days, and can be restored from there. After 30 days they are permanently removed. Only Owners and Admins can delete a project.

## Exporting project data

Open **Project settings > Export** and choose CSV or JSON. The export includes tasks, assignees, due dates, and comments, but not file attachments. We email you a download link, which is valid for 7 days.
