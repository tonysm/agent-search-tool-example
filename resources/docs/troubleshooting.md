# Troubleshooting

Fixes for the most common problems. If none of these help, contact support at support@acmeprojects.example.

## Not receiving emails

1. Check your spam or junk folder.
2. Add notifications@acmeprojects.example to your contacts or allowlist.
3. Check **Profile > Notifications** to confirm the email type is turned on.
4. Ask your IT team whether your company's mail server is blocking us.

## Unable to sign in

Confirm that you're using the email address you registered with, then try resetting your password. If your workspace enforces single sign-on, use **Sign in with SSO**. After 10 failed attempts, your account is locked for 15 minutes.

## Invitation link not working

Invitations expire after 7 days and work only for the email address they were sent to. Ask an Owner or Admin to resend the invitation from **Settings > Members > Pending invitations**.

## Can't find a project

The project may be archived, deleted, or not shared with you. Check **Settings > Archived projects** and **Settings > Trash**. If it's in neither place, ask a workspace Admin to confirm your access.

## File upload failing

Check that the file is within your plan's attachment limit: 25 MB on Free and 100 MB on Team and Business. Executable files such as `.exe` are blocked. If the file is within the limit, try a different browser or disable browser extensions.

## Pages loading slowly or not updating

Refresh the page, then clear your browser cache. Acme Projects supports the latest two versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. Current service status is available at status.acmeprojects.example.

## Workspace is read-only

A workspace becomes read-only after failed payments or when it exceeds the Free plan's limits after a cancellation. The Owner can fix this from **Settings > Billing**. See the Billing article for details.
