# Getting Started

Acme Projects is a project-management app that helps teams plan work, track tasks, and hit deadlines.

## Creating your account

1. Go to the sign-up page and enter your name, work email, and a password.
2. Open the confirmation email and click **Verify email**. The link expires after 24 hours.
3. Choose a name for your workspace. You can change it later under **Settings > Workspace**.

New accounts start on a 14-day free trial of the Team plan. No credit card is required.

## Understanding workspaces

A workspace is the top-level container for your team's projects, members, and billing. Most companies need only one. If you belong to several, switch between them from the workspace menu in the top-left corner.

## Creating your first project

1. Click **New project** on the dashboard.
2. Enter a project name and choose a template, or pick **Blank project**.
3. Click **Create**, then add your first tasks.

See the Projects and Tasks article for more on organizing work.

## Inviting your team

Open **Settings > Members** and click **Invite members**. Enter one or more email addresses, choose a role, and send the invitations. See the Team and Roles article for what each role can do.

## Setting up notifications

Open **Profile > Notifications** to choose which events email you. You can receive emails immediately or as a daily digest sent at 8:00 AM in your time zone.
