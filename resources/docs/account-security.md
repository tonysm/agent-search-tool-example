# Account Security

These settings protect your personal account and your team's workspace.

## Changing your password

Open **Profile > Security > Change password**, enter your current password and a new one, and click **Save**. Passwords must be at least 12 characters. Changing your password signs you out of all other sessions.

## Resetting a forgotten password

On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?** and enter your email. We send a reset link that expires after 1 hour. If the email does not arrive, see the Troubleshooting article.

## Enabling two-factor authentication

1. Open **Profile > Security > Two-factor authentication** and click **Enable**.
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app.
3. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm.
4. Save your recovery codes somewhere safe. Each code works only once.

Owners can require two-factor authentication for everyone under **Settings > Security**.

## Recovering access without your authenticator

On the two-factor sign-in screen, click **Use a recovery code** and enter one of your saved codes. If you have no recovery codes left, ask a workspace Owner or Admin to reset two-factor authentication for you from **Settings > Members**.

## Reviewing active sessions

Open **Profile > Security > Active sessions** to see where you are signed in. Click **Sign out** next to any session you don't recognize, then change your password.

## Setting up single sign-on

Single sign-on (SSO) is available on the Business plan. The Owner can connect a SAML identity provider under **Settings > Security > Single sign-on**. You need your provider's metadata URL. Once SSO is enforced, members must sign in through your identity provider.
