# Billing

Only the workspace Owner can view and change billing settings. All billing pages are under **Settings > Billing**.

## Comparing plans

- **Free**: Up to 5 members and 3 active projects, with 25 MB attachments.
- **Team**: $12 per member per month, or $120 per member per year. Includes unlimited projects, 100 MB attachments, and up to 20 guests.
- **Business**: $24 per member per month, or $240 per member per year. Includes everything in Team, plus single sign-on and audit logs.

## Upgrading or downgrading your plan

Open **Settings > Billing > Change plan** and choose a plan. Upgrades take effect immediately, and we charge a prorated amount for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Updating your payment method

1. Open **Settings > Billing > Payment method**.
2. Click **Replace card** and enter the new card details.
3. Click **Save**. We use the new card for your next charge.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Annual Business plans can also pay by bank transfer.

## Finding invoices

Open **Settings > Billing > Invoices** to view or download past invoices as PDFs. To add a tax ID or change the billing address on future invoices, click **Edit billing details**.

## Fixing a failed payment

If a charge fails, we email the Owner and retry after 3, 5, and 7 days. If every retry fails, the workspace becomes read-only until the balance is paid. Update your payment method and click **Pay now** on the Billing page to restore access right away.

## Canceling your subscription

Open **Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription**. Your workspace keeps its paid features until the end of the billing period, then moves to the Free plan. If it exceeds the Free plan's limits, existing projects stay read-only until you upgrade or archive enough of them.

## Requesting a refund

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refundable. To request a refund, email billing@acmeprojects.example with your workspace name.
