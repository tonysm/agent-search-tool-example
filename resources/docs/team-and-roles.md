# Team and Roles

Every workspace member has one of four roles, which determines what they can see and change.

## Comparing member roles

- **Owner**: Full access, including billing and deleting the workspace. Each workspace has one Owner.
- **Admin**: Manages members, projects, and workspace settings. Admins cannot access billing or delete the workspace.
- **Member**: Creates projects and works on tasks in any project they can access.
- **Guest**: Sees and comments on only the projects they are invited to. Guests cannot create projects.

## Inviting members

Owners and Admins can invite people from **Settings > Members > Invite members**. Invitations expire after 7 days. To send a new one, find the person under **Pending invitations** and click **Resend**.

## Changing a member's role

Open **Settings > Members**, find the person, and choose a new role from the menu next to their name. Owners can change any role. Admins can change the roles of Members and Guests only.

## Removing a member

Open **Settings > Members**, click the menu next to the person's name, and choose **Remove from workspace**. Their tasks stay in place but become unassigned. Removing a paid member frees that seat immediately, and the change appears on your next invoice.

## Transferring workspace ownership

Only the current Owner can do this. Open **Settings > Workspace > Transfer ownership**, choose an existing Admin, and confirm with your password. You become an Admin after the transfer.

## Sharing a project with a guest

Open the project, click **Share**, enter the guest's email, and click **Invite**. Guests do not count as paid seats. The Team plan allows up to 20 guests per workspace.
